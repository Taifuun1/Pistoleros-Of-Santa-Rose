
# Tileset Classification

Numbered rows indicate the tileset row. Grass is always passable. Rocks, cliffs, and forests are not.

## Grasslands

1. First three tiles are grass tiles, rest are forest. The last tile is a tall impassable cliff.
2. First three tiles are forest
3. Forest

The rest of the rows are houses, fences, powerlines etc. Rows 9 and 11 are water. Row 10 is swamp, which needs to recognized as its separate tile.

## Rocky Hills

1. Grass
2. Impassable rocks
3. Impassable rocks and cliffs
4. Grass
5. Impassable rocks with minerals in them
6. Water

## Snowy Mountains

1. Snowy ground
2. Impassable rocks
3. First four are tents, next 5 are forest and rocks
4. Impassable cliffs and water
5. Water
6. Power lines

## Prairie

1. Grass
2. Rocks
3. Forests and impassable cliffs
