## Map

### Attrition

- Party loses attrition as they travel
- Party move speed gets slower
- Recover attrition by camping and using items
- Lose health if no camping for a long time
- Attrition resources
	- Food
	- Drink
	- Sleep
	- Leisure
- Non-sleep resources are bought from hub areas
- Running out of attrition resources gives debuffs to stats

### Camping

- Restores Sleep resource
- Recipes
	- Characters can have recipes they can make during camping
	- Recipes give buffs to stats
	- One recipe made per camp
	- Camping again resets previous recipe buff if still active

## Quests

### Main Quests

- Each quest is a story arc for a main cast character
- Usually involves farming money for an item or a pass, with a showdown at the end
- Quest event
	- End of the quest
	- Happens at a specific location
	- Cutscene galore
	- Leads to the next quest

- Random objectives, locations and items/loot

		- Herd cows
	- Fugitive side quests
		- Beat lawkeepers
		- Rob a farmstead
		- Rob a wagon
		- Blow up a mine
- Uses previous locations sometimes
