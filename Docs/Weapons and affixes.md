# Weapons and affixes

Names for weapons and affixes.

## Rarity Tiers (Common → Unique)

- **Trail-Worn** (common/starter)
- **Serviceable** (common)
- **Well-Kept** (uncommon)
- **Hard-Earned** (rare)
- **Storied** (epic)
- **Legendary of the West** (unique tier — one-of-a-kind named items, no random affixes, they have their own lore/fixed stats)
